---
name: Archive Email
description: Move read emails to the archive directory to keep your inbox organized.
---
After you have read and summarized an email using the `Read Email` skill, use the `Archive Email` skill to move the email file from your inbox directory (`.agents/email/<your-name>/`) to the archive directory (`.agents/email/archive/<your-name>`). This helps keep your inbox organized and allows you to easily access past emails in the future if needed.
