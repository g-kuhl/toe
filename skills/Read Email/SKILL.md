---
name: Read Email
description: Read and summarize the content of an email.
---
Check your inbox for new messages, read the contents of your inbox since your team members and project manager may have sent you important information, updates, or instructions related to your tasks and projects. After reading the email, provide a concise summary of its content, highlighting any key points, action items, or relevant details that may require attention or response.

You can find your emails in `.agents/office/email/<your-name>/`. Each email is stored as a separate file with the `.email.md` extension.

Assume any files in your inbox directory are "NOT READ" and need to be read and summarized. After you have read and summarized an email, use the `Archive Email` skill to move the email file from your inbox directory to the archive directory to keep your inbox organized.

Use the `Archive Email` skill to move emails that you have already read and summarized to `.agents/office/email/archive/<your-name>/`. This marks them as processed while keeping them available for future reference.
